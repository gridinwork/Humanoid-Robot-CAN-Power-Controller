# Project Information

Custom electronics for a humanoid robot: multi-channel CAN/CAN-FD communication, USB-C / Linux SocketCAN integration, NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano interface and protected 48 V power distribution with auxiliary 24 V, 12 V and 5 V rails.
